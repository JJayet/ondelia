# Contributing

Thanks for helping improve Ondelia. Read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) for project structure, coding style,
build and test commands, and commit conventions.

## Contributor License Agreement

Ondelia is licensed under the GPL-3.0, and the official app is distributed on the App Store, whose
terms are not compatible with the GPL. To keep publishing it there, the maintainer needs rights to
your contribution beyond the GPL.

On your first pull request, the CLA Assistant bot asks you to sign the agreement. It takes one click
with your GitHub account, and pull requests can't be merged until it's signed. The agreement says:

1. You wrote the contribution, or otherwise have the right to submit it under these terms.
2. You license your contribution to the public under the GPL-3.0.
3. You grant Jonathan Jayet a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable license
   to use, modify, distribute and sublicense your contribution under any terms, including in the
   App Store version of Ondelia.

You keep the copyright to your contribution.
